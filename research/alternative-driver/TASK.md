# Alternative Driver Research — Technical Task

## Goal
Preserve the long-term independent GV100 KMD/UMD direction without making it an MVP dependency.

## Research only
- GV100 hardware documentation and firmware interfaces.
- Linux KMD architecture.
- Windows WDDM/MCDM architecture.
- GPUVM, command submission, synchronization and recovery.
- CAL/HAL/OSAL reuse boundaries.

## Rules
- No production dependency from Driver Fixer on this directory.
- No proprietary NVIDIA source, binaries or headers.
- Hardware claims require primary-source evidence or physical validation.

## Acceptance
Research produces documented contracts, experiments and references rather than speculative implementation.
